## Meaning / 含义

Russia — icons and winter: gilt, liturgical red, birch white, slate blue over snow. Cultural liturgy, not the flag alone.

俄罗斯——圣像与冬天：鎏金、礼仪红、桦白、雪上石板蓝。礼仪文化，非仅国旗。
