# Maiden

group: minor

## Meaning / 含义

Maiden — young woman; also the romantic, delicate sense of “maidenly.” Stationery warmth: soft paper surfaces with readable ink.

Maiden（少女）——年轻女性，亦含「少女气」的浪漫细腻。信笺般的温暖：柔软纸面配可读墨色。

## Light Maiden

### English

Warm shell pink (low-sat red, high lightness) with deep rose-red body text and golden-yellow highlights. Olive-green muted marks add a vintage stationery feel — soft surface, strong readable ink.

### 中文

浅色：暖贝壳粉（低饱和红、高明度），正文深红玫瑰，高光金黄。橄榄绿弱标记带复古信笺感——面软、墨足、可读。

## Dark Maiden

### English

Ink and wax by lamplight: aged vellum umber shadow, iron-gall rose ink text, honey-gold highlights, dried-sage muted marks. Literary, restrained, pre-modern femininity.

### 中文

深色：灯下墨与蜡——陈年羊皮纸棕影、五倍子玫瑰墨正文、蜜金高光、干鼠尾草绿弱色。文学、克制、前现代的女性气质。
