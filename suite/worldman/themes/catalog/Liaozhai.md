# Liaozhai

group: vibe

## Meaning / 含义

聊斋 / Liaozhai (Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio) — xuan paper, cinnabar seal, jade fox-fire, moonlit ink.

聊斋——宣纸、朱砂印、狐火翠玉、月下墨色；志怪与古典文言的夜色。

## Light Liaozhai

### English

Xuan-paper cream shell, ink-black text, cinnabar seal actions, jade intervals, lantern-gold glow — classical ghost-romance by day.

### 中文

浅色：宣纸奶油壳、墨黑正文、朱砂印主操作、翠玉间隔、灯笼金辉——白日里的古典志怪。

## Dark Liaozhai

### English

Moon ink wash: near-black indigo night, fox-fire jade cursor, cinnabar blood accents, pale paper text — studio tales after midnight.

### 中文

深色：月下墨洗——近黑靛蓝夜、狐火翠光标、朱砂血强调、淡纸正文——子夜书斋里的志怪。
