# Tomboy

group: minor

## Meaning / 含义

Tomboy — sporty casual confidence: denim blue, khaki olive, construction-orange accents. Little pink; practical energy.

Tomboy（假小子）——运动休闲自信：丹宁蓝、卡其橄榄、工程橙强调。少粉；务实能量。
