# LGBTQ

group: minor

## Meaning / 含义

Colors from the Gilbert Baker rainbow pride flag as accents — celebratory without painting the whole UI in stripes.

取自吉尔伯特·贝克彩虹旗的色带作强调——欢庆，但不要把整界面刷成彩条。

## Light LGBTQ

### English

Neutral cool-gray shell; stripe hues as readable accents (red emphasis, orange/yellow highlights, green strings, blue intervals, violet actions, cyan glow).

### 中文

浅色：中性冷灰壳；彩虹色带作可读强调（红强调、橙/黄高光、绿串、蓝间隔、紫主操作、青辉光）。

## Dark LGBTQ

### English

Rain-slick night parade: deep storm-indigo shell so stripe hues glow like wet neon — celebration needs darkness as backdrop, not as mute.

### 中文

深色：雨夜游行——深风暴靛蓝壳托住彩带，如湿霓虹发光；欢庆需要暗作背景，而非把色彩闷死。
