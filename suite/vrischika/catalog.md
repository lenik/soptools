# Vrischika demo theme catalog

Twenty concrete vertical-slice themes.

- **`godot-rpg2d_topdown`** — Lantern Cartography / 灯笼制图师: A junior cartographer maps a flooded district before the tide seals the archives.
- **`godot-stg2d_side`** — Paper Crane Sortie / 纸鹤出击: Side-scrolling bullet hell where origami cranes dogfight factory smog-drones.
- **`godot-stg2d_topdown`** — Lotus Barrage / 莲华弹幕: Top-down STG in a temple pond where koi spirits duel a corrupted bell.
- **`godot-act2d_side`** — Rooftop Pickpocket / 屋脊妙手: Side-view parkour thief racing rooftops before the watch lanterns rise.
- **`godot-pzl2d`** — Ink Well Gates / 墨井之门: Slide-and-stamp puzzle: pour ink to open seals in a sealed scriptorium.
- **`godot-rts2d_topdown`** — Ember Outpost / 余烬哨站: Tiny top-down RTS: hold a mountain pass with three squad types against nightly raids.
- **`unity-fps3d`** — Frostglass Corridor / 霜璃廊道: FPS clear of a cryo-lab where glassed specimens still move behind frost.
- **`unity-rpg2d_topdown`** — Market of Echo Masks / 回声面具市集: Top-down RPG where every purchased mask permanently changes dialogue trees.
- **`unity-act2d_side`** — Neon Rail Escape / 霓虹轨逃: Side-scrolling runner/parkour escape along a collapsing maglev.
- **`unity-act3d`** — Ashline Pursuit / 灰线追逃: 3D parkour/escape through a burning transit hub while a drone net closes.
- **`unity-td2d_topdown`** — Spore Bastion / 孢子堡垒: Tower defense: fungi towers vs. migrating beetle waves on a greenhouse roof.
- **`unity-exp3d`** — Salt Chapel Walk / 盐堂漫步: Immersive walkthrough of a coastal chapel that rebuilds as you uncover diaries.
- **`unity-exp3d_vr`** — Orbital Attic VR / 轨道阁楼 VR: VR free-roam in a space-habitat attic of floating heirlooms.
- **`unity-rts3d_iso`** — Terrace War Gardens / 梯田战园: Isometric RTS on flooded rice terraces with water-level as a strategic resource.
- **`unity-rce3d`** — Monsoon Relay / 季风接力: Arcade racer: monsoon mountain relay with standing water and landslide shortcuts.
- **`unreal-fps3d`** — Volt Foundry Breach / 伏特铸造突袭: Unreal FPS in a vertical foundry with live molten lanes and electromagnetic traps.
- **`unreal-exp3d`** — Cathedral of Weather / 气象大教堂: Large-scene immersive roam: a cathedral whose stained glass drives volumetric weather.
- **`unreal-act3d`** — Chain-Hook District / 链钩街区: 3D action: swing-hook traversal across a collapsing dock district.
- **`bevy-act2d_side`** — Clockwork Gull / 发条海鸥: Bevy side-action: wind-up seagull courier dodging pier hazards.
- **`bevy-rpg2d_topdown`** — Moth Library / 飞蛾图书馆: Bevy top-down mini-RPG in a library where moths eat unread quests.
